I am a PhD student in Computer Science at [UCLA](https://www.ucla.edu/), where I work with [Prof. Jason Cong](https://vast.cs.ucla.edu/people/faculty/jason-cong) in the VAST research group. I am broadly interested in mathematical optimization problems in electronic design automation (EDA). My recent work spans quantum circuit synthesis, logic synthesis, and LLM agents for hardware design-space exploration.

Before joining UCLA, I received my M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology from ETH Zurich, where I worked with [Prof. Lana Josipović](https://dynamo.ethz.ch/) in the DYNAMO group and with [Prof. Giovanni De Micheli](https://si2.epfl.ch/~demichel/) at the Integrated Systems Laboratory, EPFL. I completed my B.Eng. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the [UM-SJTU Joint Institute](https://www.ji.sjtu.edu.cn/), Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I worked with [Prof. Weikang Qian](https://umji.sjtu.edu.cn/~wkqian/people/weikang-qian.html) on logic synthesis.
