**Hanyu Wang, Chang Meng, Giovanni De Micheli** · IWLS 2024

## Summary

Logic synthesis quality depends heavily on the sequence of optimization passes (the "recipe") applied to a circuit. This work explores an evolution strategy for logic synthesis in which candidate solutions are recombined through a *global crossover* operator, exchanging information across entire optimization trajectories rather than making only local moves. The approach was presented at the 33rd International Workshop on Logic & Synthesis (IWLS 2024).
