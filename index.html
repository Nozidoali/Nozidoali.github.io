I am a PhD student in Computer Science at UCLA, where I work with Prof. Jason Cong in the VAST research group. I am broadly interested in mathematical optimization problems in electronic design automation (EDA). My recent work spans quantum circuit synthesis, logic synthesis, and LLM agents for hardware design-space exploration.

Before joining UCLA, I received my M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology from ETH Zurich, where I worked with Prof. Lana Josipović in the DYNAMO group and with Prof. Giovanni De Micheli at the Integrated Systems Laboratory, EPFL. I completed my B.Eng. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the UM-SJTU Joint Institute, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I worked with Prof. Weikang Qian on logic synthesis.
