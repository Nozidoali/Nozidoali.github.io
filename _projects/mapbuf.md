MapBuf integrates buffer placement with technology mapping for dataflow circuits generated by high-level synthesis, so that buffer insertion decisions see the true post-mapping timing. It was nominated for the Best Paper Award at both IWLS and ICCAD 2023 {% cite wang2023mapbuf %}, and is integrated into the [Dynamatic](https://dynamatic.epfl.ch/) HLS compiler.

[Code on GitHub](https://github.com/Nozidoali/MapBuf).
