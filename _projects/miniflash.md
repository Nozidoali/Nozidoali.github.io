MiniFlash compiles Clifford+T quantum circuits into lattice-surgery operations for surface-code fault-tolerant quantum computers. It focuses on compilation speed and resource efficiency, translating logical circuits into schedules of merges and splits on the surface-code lattice.

[Code on GitHub](https://github.com/Nozidoali/MiniFlash).
