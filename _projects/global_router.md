A global router built for the ECE 556 contest at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, combining attention-inspired net ordering with heterogeneous rip-up and re-routing. It won 1st place in the 2020 contest with the lowest total wire length. [Code on GitHub](https://github.com/Nozidoali/Attention-based-Global-Router).
